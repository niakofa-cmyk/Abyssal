# Abyssal — storytelling and world blueprint

The ocean is the evidence. The ruins are the history. The diver is the investigator. The climb toward the surface is the question.

This is Abyssal's own mythology: the Aurelian Grid, the pressure heart, the shelf districts, the Lantern Maw, the collar that stays shut. It borrows a method — discover a dead civilization by swimming through it — and does not borrow another game's plot, people, places, or pictures.

The campaign order is [CAMPAIGN.md](./CAMPAIGN.md). A is the warning. B is the lantern's history, earned in stages. C, the surface, stays sealed until the ascent is a chapter instead of a menu.

## What the code does now

- Six kinds of station sit on the desks that were already in the water, plus one personal note in the breach loft beside the first air.
- Three investigations — the pressure heart, the city that drank, what followed the heat — reconstruct only when every piece is known.
- The timeline lights a beat when any of its records are known. The first beat is simply waking up.
- What you swam to, you can read. What another diver recovered, you can see as a title until you reach that desk.
- A case can close for the whole expedition when no single diver has read every piece. That shared list persists with the ocean. It is not a new database column.
- Understanding is unchanged. The loft canister is still the first order. A personal note does not count as "not only an accident."

## The opening

The diver does not begin with the truth.

They wake under a dead grid, short on air, with no lecture. Warning is the water, the dark, and the desks that still glow. The first climb is for air. The loft glass has a name and a promise of oranges. The black box, the unsent evacuation, and the unsigned specimen note disagree with one another. Later, the works say the shut order was written over twice.

The player is not told that a people are gone, what the heart was for, or what waits above the collar. Those are the things the dive earns.

## Stations

A station is a glowing desk. Swimming into it files the record. It is not a menu.

| Kind | What it is for | Already filed |
|---|---|---|
| History | The shelf before the dark | Census, tram log |
| Emergency | The hours of the blast, and they can contradict | Unsent evacuation |
| Research | The heart, the pipes, the life that moved first | Black box, specimen note, lantern card, reef watch |
| Engineering | How the grid, the hulls, and the locks worked | Collar probe, Barrow chit, refused upper lock |
| Personal | A person, not a report | The name in the loft glass |
| Classified | The line that was not supposed to be read | The overwritten furnace order |

Some records are damaged on purpose. One desk is not the truth. A reconstructed investigation is the truth the expedition can currently stand on, and a later desk can change it.

## The three investigations

1. **The pressure heart.** Black box, unsent evacuation, furnace order. The question is whether the blackout was only a failed plant. The reconstruction says the heart had a second line, the shut order was rewritten, and the vent was the last thing the logger heard.
2. **The city that drank.** Census, tram log, unsent evacuation. The shelf was lived in. The tram left the school empty. The order to run was never sent.
3. **What followed the heat.** Specimen note, lantern card, reef watch. The eels left the lights before the blast. The anemones rooted on the old heat. The Maw followed that heat. It is not a random mouth.

Killing the Maw still opens the shelf door. The archive does not add a second condition.

## What stays personal, and what the expedition shares

- Your journal holds the words you swam to.
- The expedition holds the list of records anyone has recovered.
- Another diver's record shows as a title until you read the desk yourself.
- When the last piece of a case comes up, every diver in the water hears that the records agree.
- A reconnect keeps your place, your own readings, and the shared list.

## Creatures

They are habitat, not a target list. The notes already say how each one lives: darters panic, eels commit after the lure steadies, hunters prefer hulls, sentries telegraph, anemones own a circle, the Maw shares one body across every diver in the plains. The warmth investigation is the story reason the Maw is there.

## What this blueprint refuses

- Nine new regions in one pass.
- A click-to-read computer that explains the plot.
- A lock that waits on a completed archive.
- Another game's history, with the names changed.
- Combat as the only way a discovery matters.

## Later, only after this expedition is the one a diver remembers

A playable blast instead of a waking. More personal records. A damaged archive that needs all four pieces before a sentence appears. Power that, once restored, wakes something that was contained. The trench, then the collar, then whatever the surface actually is. None of that is this pass.
