# Abyssal — campaign order

A first. B beside it. C only when the climb has been earned.

The surface is the promise of the dive, not a mode on the menu. Reaching it should feel like crossing into another world. Letting a diver switch between the ocean and the surface from the first minute would spend that ending before the ocean has meant anything.

This is not a second game. The same diver, the same beacon, the same archive, and the same ocean carry through. Nothing here opens the trench, the upper ocean, or the surface.

## A — The warning

The diver starts inside the Aurelian Grid, not already lost in the dark.

1. The heart reading is steady. Walk the gantry. A is left. D is right.
2. The reading spikes. The evacuation lock is east.
3. That door seals. The gantry takes water. You do not outrun it.
4. The blast. Something large had already crossed the east lock. You do not know its name.
5. You wake in the breach, short on air. The first order is still the loft.

Returning to a dive you already hold skips the warning. A new diver can skip it, but the ocean does not start there.

You wake with the shape already gone. In the blast it had a light. Over the breach, once, it passes and does not stop. Two watches in the wreck disagree: a loose hull, or something that turned toward the heart. Either record files the first lantern stage. Neither one changes the loft order or closes a case.

The surface is a look, not a region. The first time you read the collar probe or the refused upper lock, the water goes pale, the drowned platforms and the half-sunk coastal grid are there, and then the collar shuts. You are still where you were. Nothing above can be walked.

## B — The lantern, in stages

The Maw is not introduced as a health bar. The archive keeps five stages. Only four can be earned now. The fifth stays dark on purpose.

| Stage | When it lights | What it is willing to say |
|---|---|---|
| Signs | The sonar return in the breach | It was crossing the east lock before the heart failed |
| Known | The field card in the plains | The watch already knew the lure, and not to chase it |
| Heat | The specimen note, or the reef watch | The eels left first. It followed the heat |
| Mouth | You have seen it | The open mouth is the honest place. It can be watched, drawn, or shared |
| Why | A record that does not exist yet | Why it holds the shelf is not in the files |

The fight in the plains is still the shared encounter. Killing it still opens the shelf door and nothing else. Smaller life still reacts as it already does: darters panic, eels commit after the lure, hunters prefer hulls.

## C — The surface, later

Not a selectable mode. A chapter, after the underwater expedition is the thing a diver remembers.

What the ocean may show before that day:

- A pale band of light at the top of the reef. Not an exit.
- The collar probe and the refused upper lock, already in the archive.
- The chart listing the Surface Frontier as sealed.

What the first arrival should be, when it is built:

The water goes brighter. The sound changes. The diver breaks the surface and finds an ocean horizon with no rescue on it. Rusted platforms and pylons stand out of the water, and the streets continue underneath them. Farther off, a coastal city is half drowned, towers greened over, storm light instead of a welcome. Closer wrecks are skeletal, a bridge still standing in the fog, no one on it. The picture of the ascent itself is a body crossing the waterline into the sun.

That surface, when it exists, is weather, debris, and unstable decks. It reuses the diver, the journal, and the shared ocean. It does not become a second game with a second menu.

Until then the surface gives nothing you can walk on.

## What this pass did not build

A dry facility with real doors in the simulation. A new region. A surface movement model. Weather. A way to travel back down from a coast that is not there yet. The warning is a walk on a gantry, then the ocean you already know.
