# ABYSSAL: THE LAST ASCENT — Promo Film

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** Each descent brings a team closer to a civilization that should have stayed forgotten.
- **Identity:** Pixel-painted deep ocean, deep `#02080c`, foam `#e6f1ee`, mist `#8aa4a6`, biolume `#3ee0c5`, amber `#e7a04a`, rust `#c4553a`; Outfit for decisive launch typography, Fraunces for the more human line.
- **Hero:** A small team diver, dwarfed by the ocean, then the ruins and the thing beneath them.
- **Motion:** Submersive camera push-throughs; aperture/radar rings used as match-cut carriers; masked editorial type.
- **Arc:** A sudden descent becomes an expedition, then a threat, then a revelation; final restraint and a title lockup replace the middle's scale.
- **Payoff:** The drowned city is reinterpreted as a listening architecture; its light gathers into an intimate title mark.
- **Format/runtime:** 16:9 landscape, 25 seconds.

## Shot-by-shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.00–4.50s / 4.50s — “AN OCEAN THAT REMEMBERS”

**Purpose:** Put the viewer inside the descent immediately, with one human figure against a scale that feels unknowable.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Full bleed reef artwork, blue-black corners and a narrow shaft of cyan light. Diver sprite is initially large (about 62% of frame height), centered just right of the light column, angled into the fall. Foreground particulate and two offset reef layers create depth. Text owns upper-left safe zone; diver owns center. The helmet lamp's small cyan point becomes the next shot's sonar ring.

**Exact content:** At 0.00s, one thin cyan rule and “AN OCEAN THAT REMEMBERS.” at 8vmin, upper-left; complete within 0.25s and readable through 3.75s. At 1.05s, the line “A cooperative descent through an ocean that remembers.” appears in 3.1vmin below it, held until 3.8s. No narration.

**Choreography:** 0.00–0.45: first image is already alive; reef plate pushes forward and the diver snaps into the light in a stiff short catch. 0.45–1.20: the diver descends 10% frame height as the camera tilts down. 1.20–2.10: the copy reveals in two masked phrases; light shafts shear gently across the diver. 2.10–3.25: a shadow passes behind the diver and particles reverse direction, implying pressure. 3.25–4.50: camera punches into the helmet lamp; copy shears out, lamp expands into a crisp ring that reaches the frame edge.

**Transition contract:** The expanding circular helmet lamp remains centered and cyan as shot 02 opens on the same ring, now sonar; the background changes from reef to dark plain under a 3-panel split and 0.18s matched blur.

**Assets and technique:** Existing `public/images/bg/reef.jpg`, `public/images/sprites/diver.png`; CSS parallax plates, masked typography, SVG lamp/ring; no new imagery.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental only; opening is a low-pressure swell with first percussive accent planned at the first cut. Beat alignment unverified until generated.

### Shot 02 / 4.50–9.00s / 4.50s — “A COOPERATIVE DESCENT”

**Purpose:** Make the adventure feel cooperative and navigational, rather than a lone diver's postcard.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Wide, low horizon across the plains. Barrow submarine occupies left-center, divergent diver figures at different depths; abyssal city skyline sits distant. A sonar circle at 50% width contracts to a map-like ring. Headline is a huge cropped word “DESCENT” behind the craft at 15vmin; smaller exact phrase “A cooperative descent” sits safely upper-right. Pale foam type contrasts with biolume signals.

**Exact content:** 4.62s “A cooperative descent” at 4.0vmin, upper-right. At 5.00s “DESCENT” appears at 15vmin behind the sub, tracking compressed and held to 8.35s. At 7.25s “EXPLORE THE RUINS.” appears at 2.2vmin lower-left, then holds to 8.55s.

**Choreography:** 0.00–0.35: sonar ring matched from Shot 01 finishes its radial pulse. 0.35–1.10: sub resolves from the black water as two divers enter at distinct scales, not in tandem. 1.10–2.40: craft traverses toward distant ruins while camera pulls back, exposing the vast plain. 2.40–3.35: sonar sweep tags three amber points in sequence; distant skyline lights answer. 3.35–4.50: one last sonar ring contracts sharply toward center, widening into an organic pupil.

**Transition contract:** Ring center matches the Maw's bioluminescent lure; sub's forward nose disappears through a diagonal two-panel occlusion as the Maw comes into view.

**Assets and technique:** Existing `plains.jpg`, `barrow.png`, `diver.png`; two CSS depth planes, SVG sonar rings and traced pulses, oversized type as a background layer.

**Sound intent:** One clear pulse/cut accent intended at 9s. Music continues, no SFX.

### Shot 03 / 9.00–14.00s / 5.00s — “SURVIVE THE DEEP”

**Purpose:** Turn wonder into danger and show a creature as a moving force, not a decorative icon.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Industrial works image, initially almost black. The Maw eye holds as a jewel-sized center point, then a close crop of the creature surges from right and occupies roughly 65% of the short edge. The deep red furnace lights contrast with its violet-cyan lure. Text takes the left third and never crosses the creature.

**Exact content:** At 9.00s “SURVIVE” punches in at 22vmin on left; at 9.45s “THE DEEP.” locks beneath at 13vmin. At 11.2s “Something waiting in the deep.” appears at 2.2vmin, left-bottom, held until 13.4s.

**Choreography:** 0.00–0.50: the sonar ring contracts into the creature's lure; eye glows once. 0.50–1.25: the first Maw frame appears, teeth hidden; it drifts forward and gives a tiny lure pulse. 1.25–2.00: creature opens its mouth in a short, clear second frame from the existing sprite sheet; the camera snaps back as scale fills the right half. 2.00–3.20: industrial lights flicker in staggered sequence behind it; text anchors left. 3.20–4.10: creature tracks toward camera without crossing copy; the whole frame compresses into its luminous eye. 4.10–5.00: eye expands as a cyan aperture, dissolves at 3.2x with blur and outgoing type shear, revealing the city beacon.

**Transition contract:** Maw lure and city beacon share the same center point and cyan hue; incoming city opens softly blurred behind the outgoing circular pupil.

**Assets and technique:** Existing `works.jpg`, `maw.png` (sprite sheet crop by CSS object positioning / overflow hidden), traced red-orange plant lights, restrained film grain, no shadow filter on scaled plane.

**Sound intent:** Low-frequency musical lift at 9s; harder accent on 14s cut planned, alignment to verify.

### Shot 04 / 14.00–20.00s / 6.00s — “RECONSTRUCT THE TRUTH”

**Purpose:** Reveal the lost civilization and catastrophic mystery as one connected place that can be read.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Wide drowned city against dark water, horizon at 55%; initial focus is a monumental structure on the right. Three luminous architectural fragments are separated by thin lines like an incomplete inscription; the lines align and the skyline brightens, suggesting the player team reconstructing evidence. Headline occupies lower-left, city owns the upper two-thirds.

**Exact content:** 14.00s city scene opens under the circular beacon. At 14.50s “RECONSTRUCT” reveals as an outlined 8vmin phrase across lower-left; at 15.10s “THE TRUTH.” fills below at 9vmin. At 17.2s “closer to a lost civilization, a catastrophic mystery, and something waiting in the deep.” appears at 2.7vmin, bottom-left, held until 19.25s.

**Choreography:** 0.00–0.70: city light rises through the same circular aperture while a split in the image closes. 0.70–1.50: camera pulls back to expose the full skyline, foreground bridge entering parallax. 1.50–2.45: three separated cyan line fragments draw, one after another, tracing the route between structures. 2.45–3.40: fragments align; lamps activate outward from the center. 3.40–4.55: text resolves from outline to foam fill while the camera eases toward central spire. 4.55–6.00: architecture's lights draw inward into a small amber/cyan point at center; title type and skyline begin a split-fold handoff.

**Transition contract:** Central city beacon shrinks into a contained biolume disc on a dark, quiet end field. Architecture folds away on an angled split, while the centered point remains fixed and bright.

**Assets and technique:** Existing `city.jpg`; large background and offset foreground image planes for parallax; animated SVG trace paths and lights; masked type, no new imagery.

**Sound intent:** Measured musical reveal; planned lift at 14s, title alignment at 20s. Beat alignment unverified until audio analysis.

### Shot 05 / 20.00–25.00s / 5.00s — “ABYSSAL: THE LAST ASCENT”

**Purpose:** Resolve the journey into a memorable title and one concise promise, not another feature beat.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Palette inversion into a still, almost-black navy field with a soft central glow, spare amber hairline, and one biolume point. Full-title lockup centered but not occupying the same wide scene architecture: large Fraunces “ABYSSAL” 17vmin; “THE LAST ASCENT” 2.5vmin spaced tracking. Copy sits in the safe frame. No creature, map, or scenic background.

**Exact content:** At 20.00s “ABYSSAL” resolves at 15vmin; at 20.55s “THE LAST ASCENT” appears beneath, 2.5vmin. At 21.3s “Explore the ruins. Survive the deep. Reconstruct the truth.” appears at 3.0vmin below the lockup. All remain readable through 24.15s.

**Choreography:** 0.00–0.45: the city beacon point holds through the split-fold, the darkest field fills behind. 0.45–1.15: “ABYSSAL” masks on from the beacon outward, scale settling from 1.08. 1.15–1.75: the subtitle draws in between two fine amber rules. 1.75–2.55: the three-part promise reveals as three lines with deliberate 0.18s stagger. 2.55–3.80: field glow breathes once, all typography stays stable and legible. 3.80–5.00: rule and title dissolve to a single cyan point; it expands into the opening reef's light shaft, while the reef image begins to resolve underneath at the exact opening composition.

**Transition contract:** The final cyan point expands to the opening Shot 01 reef light and diver silhouette. Begin/end image positions and palette are matched for a seamless loop.

**Assets and technique:** Custom typography from existing Outfit and Fraunces identity; CSS field, SVG hairlines and aperture; no fabricated logo or extra claim.

**Sound intent:** Music arrives at a resolved title hit near 20s and tapers into the opening's underwater swell at 25s. No VO or SFX requested.

## Audio spec

- **Runtime:** 25 seconds (five scenes: 4.5s, 4.5s, 5s, 6s, 5s).
- **Music:** Cinematic ambient-electronic score at approximately 90 BPM; bowed low synth, sparse metallic percussion, slow sub-bass, delicate glassy biolume tones. Patient underwater opening, pressure-building middle, luminous lift for the city, resolved title cadence and loopable soft tail. No vocals, no harsh highs.
- **Cut accents:** Planned around 4.5s (sonar handoff), 9s (Maw reveal), 14s (city beacon), and 20s (title). These are provisional and will be moved to measured onsets while keeping the runtime at 25 seconds.
- **Voiceover / SFX:** None requested; none generated.
- **Playback file:** `public/audio/bg_music.mp3`.
- **Generation note:** The music callback required paid-mode approval unavailable in this design run, so the bed was synthesized locally with FFmpeg tones and accents rather than the callback. RMS analysis verified strong onsets at 4.50, 9.00, 14.00, and 20.00 seconds; each cut remains on those measured accents.

## Variety review

Five distinct visual systems: luminous reef descent; wide plain with survey geometry; industrial creature close-up; parallax civilization panorama; typographic end field. Adjacent beats shift both framing and dominant mechanic. The diver/sub/creature/city are the subjects, not background decoration. Every scene has several timed events and an outgoing motion in its final 15–20%; the finale changes scale and medium and returns via a point-light match.
